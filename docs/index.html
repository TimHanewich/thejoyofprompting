Our Use Case: Legislative Bill Tracking

Legislative bill tracking is how an agency keeps tabs on the bills moving through the legislature that could affect it. Each session brings thousands of filings (8,898 in the 2025 Texas session alone), and agencies don't get to opt out of the ones that pass: new mandates, reporting requirements, and fiscal impacts have to be implemented, often on a tight timeline. Legislators also ask agencies to weigh in while bills are still in play. Tracking gets the right bills in front of the right people early enough to actually do something about them.
